## Feedforward, Feed Up and More — (P)

Source: `assignmentWeek8FeedforwardFeedUp.docx` · Date: 07.06.2026
(assignment week of 01–06 / 05–06).

### Assignment 1 (group) — Rewrite a poorly defined task using *feed up*

Original task: *"Write a 1500-word essay about Professional Skills."*

Rewritten task: Write a 1500-word (±10%) reflective essay on professional skills in
IT/AI. **Goal:** show you can name the skills that matter, explain why they matter, and
reflect on your own growth. **Reader:** your tutor and your future self before an
internship. The essay should:
- Define "professional skills" and focus on 3–4 in depth (e.g. communication, teamwork,
  time management, ethics).
- Explain why each one matters in IT/AI, with a concrete example.
- Reflect on where you are now and how you will improve.
- Use a clear structure.
- Use at least 3 reliable sources in APA style.
- Format: 1500 words ±10% (excl. references), PDF, deadline set by tutor.

### Assignment 2 (pair) — Give only *feedforward*

1. **Pair's challenge (one sentence):** To involve other team members in my work and wait
   for them to show up to work together, rather than just working alone.
2. **Feedforward (≥2 concrete suggestions):**
   - Communicate your work as much as possible; give clear explanations so others can
     keep up with your process.
   - Look into collaboration tools (Trello, GitHub Issues, Linear) and the Scrum
     processes that go with them.

### Assignment 3 (individual) — Reflection and Transfer (≤250 words)

- **Where do I need more feed up in my studies?** A clearer overview of everything to
  complete during the period would help. I make my own checklist planner, but it's often
  difficult because assignments are confusing at first glance, change over time, or new
  ones are added as the period goes on.
- **How can I ask for feedforward effectively?** Know where you need it: after finishing
  something, identify the parts you struggled with and seek feedforward on those — ideally
  from someone with more experience in that area.
- **How will I use feedforward in group work?** Use it as much as possible and bring up
  members where I can, increasing the group's working potential — which in turn makes my
  own work easier.
